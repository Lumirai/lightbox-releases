# Lumirai Lightbox

Lumirai Lightbox enables the secure, efficient, and engaging display of relevant medical imaging
in an exam room. Using your schedule and room assignments, it automatically pre-loads the relevant
studies for the patient in each room from your Medstrat Joints PACS. Images remain hidden behind a
screensaver until the provider unlocks the display using their proximity card.

A Lumirai subscription and site setup are required for all functionality, including room
coordination and EMR/PACS integration.

## How it works

1. Your clinic staff rooms a patient in your EMR or Lumirai console.
2. Lumirai Lightbox automatically pre-loads images from your PACS in the room.
3. Tap your NFC-based ID card to unlock and display the images.

Pair it with Lumirai Viewer on a tablet to preview studies before entering the room.

[More about Lightbox](https://www.lumir.ai/lightbox)

## Download for Windows

**[Lightbox for Windows (x64)](https://github.com/Lumirai/lightbox-releases/releases/latest/download/Lightbox-Setup-x64.exe)**
for most PCs, or
**[Lightbox for Windows (ARM)](https://github.com/Lumirai/lightbox-releases/releases/latest/download/Lightbox-Setup-arm64.exe)**
for Snapdragon and Surface Pro ARM. Run the installer; you can choose “only for me” or “anyone who
uses this computer.” Builds are signed by Lumirai LLC, and Lightbox keeps itself up to date from there.
If needed, you can disable automatic updates in the settings panel.

Lightbox can also be [downloaded and updated from the Microsoft App Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9p4j9t0tctss?hl=en-US&gl=US&ocid=pdpshare).
